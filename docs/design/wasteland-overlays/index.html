Merged-chat design

  1. Variant: A1 compact (your pick), restyled as a bunker-terminal capsule.
  2. Typeface: Oswald, 700 for usernames and 500 for messages (Google Fonts, OFL license).
  3. Row colors:
    • fill #0F3B33 at 94%
    • border #D4B02A, 4 px, with a #12110D outline, 2 px
    • username #F2D22E
    • message #F3EAD3
    • removed-message text #A9C2BA
  4. Texture: generated in code from SVG noise, with no image files. The fill has dark grunge and faint horizontal scratches. Yellow spray flecks sit near both ends and along the bottom edge, and dark chips break up the border. Each row shifts the chips so no two rows look the same.
  5. Emblem: an original orbit-and-bolt mark: one tilted orbit ring with a lightning bolt through it. It has no gear or "winged circle" shape. The same mark appears on the chat rows, the bunker doors and the flag in the playground.
  6. Platform cues: YouTube #E5322D uses a play tile, Twitch #9146FF a speech square, and Kick #53FC18 a notched K block. All are original glyphs.
  7. Motion: A new row opens over 180 ms, then slides 28 px and fades in over 240 ms with ease-out. The oldest row fades and rises 8 px over 220 ms. With reduced motion, rows only fade.
  8. Removed messages: the text changes to "message removed by a moderator" in muted italics, stays for 1.4 s, then the row collapses over 240 ms.
  9. Overflow: usernames longer than 20 characters end in an ellipsis. Messages wrap inside the row and never truncate. Emotes are 1.1 em inline, and emoji are native.

B. Character playground

The bottom 360 px of the frame, with a transparent top. The floor is a catwalk of rusty girders, which cannot be broken, and dry dirt, which can, with cables, pillars and a lamp hanging underneath. Viewers materialize in the JOIN door and dematerialize in the EXIT door. Anyone who falls or goes boom comes back on the respawn pad.

1080p frame, scaled

Commands use the selected sender. Offscreen senders wait for admission, enter, and then act. Digging uses the ground beneath that sender. On dirt, the tile goes from intact to cracked 1 to cracked 2 to a hole, and rubble falls out. On a girder, digging gives the "unavailable" feedback: a clang spark, a red × and a short bubble. Holes and temporary stairs last 30 seconds. Falling or disappearing in a dust poof leads to a 2.5-second respawn delay.

Confirmed playground rules

  1. Movement: continuous walking. After the configured timeout, head to the exit. A message turns an inactivity exit back toward the center.
  2. Activity: 60-second default, configurable. Any message resets the timer. Offscreen senders enter before their command runs.
  3. Crowd: at most 40 visible: 30 ranked and 10 rotating. Recent messages determine rank; rapid spam adds no score. Rotation gives occasional chatters a turn.
  4. Terrain: only dirt breaks. Protected metal remains solid. Holes and stairs affect everyone and expire after 30 seconds.
  5. Respawn: return after 2.5 seconds if still eligible. Commands affect the sender; no targeted attacks.
  6. Artwork: one original copper-helmet courier, canvas workwear, dark boots and platform-colored scarf/vest. No green hair, vault suit, franchise numbers or game insignia.
  7. Theme: wasteland only. Art and animation mapping remain separate from game rules so later themes can replace them.
  8. Preview limits: local synthetic events only. Channel authorization, moderation delivery, durable state, and account isolation belong to #662 and #664.
