Decisions to approve: merged chat

  1. Variant: A1 compact (your pick), restyled as a bunker-terminal capsule. A2 has been removed.
  2. Typeface: Oswald, 700 for usernames and 500 for messages (Google Fonts, OFL license). It is the closest match to reference 1, which uses a tall, heavy, condensed face with a large x-height. I also tried Barlow Condensed 800, but it reads wider and rounder.
  3. Row colors:
    • fill #0F3B33 at 94%
    • border #D4B02A, 4 px, with a #12110D outline, 2 px
    • username #F2D22E
    • message #F3EAD3
    • removed-message text #A9C2BA
  4. Texture: generated in code from SVG noise, with no image files. The fill has dark grunge and faint horizontal scratches. Yellow spray flecks sit near both ends and along the bottom edge, and dark chips break up the border. Each row shifts the chips so no two rows look the same.
  5. Emblem: an original orbit-and-bolt mark: one tilted orbit ring with a lightning bolt through it. It has no gear or "winged circle" shape. A two-orbit atom was tried and turned to mush at pixel scale. The same mark appears on the chat rows, the bunker doors and the flag in the playground.
  6. Platform cues: unchanged. YouTube #E5322D uses a play tile, Twitch #9146FF a speech square, and Kick #53FC18 a notched K block. All are original glyphs.
  7. Motion: unchanged. A new row opens over 180 ms, then slides 28 px and fades in over 240 ms with ease-out. The oldest row fades and rises 8 px over 220 ms. With reduced motion, rows only fade.
  8. Removed messages: the text changes to "message removed by a moderator" in muted italics, stays for 1.4 s, then the row collapses over 240 ms.
  9. Overflow: usernames longer than 20 characters end in an ellipsis. Messages wrap inside the row and never truncate. Emotes are 1.1 em inline, and emoji are native.
Issue #664

B. Character playground

The bottom 360 px of the frame, with a transparent top. The floor is a catwalk of rusty girders, which cannot be broken, and dry dirt, which can, with cables, pillars and a lamp hanging underneath. Viewers materialize in the JOIN door and dematerialize in the EXIT door. Anyone who falls or goes boom comes back on the respawn pad.

1080p frame, scaled

Each command runs on a random free character, who digs wherever they are standing. On dirt, the tile goes from intact to cracked 1 to cracked 2 to a hole, and rubble falls out. On a girder, digging gives the "unavailable" feedback: a clang spark, a red × and a short bubble. For this demo, holes start repairing after about 10 s, running back through the stages, and staircases crumble after about 9 s.

Decisions to approve: character playground

  1. Sprite size: the character is 20×26 logical px inside a 28×28 frame. The spare room is for tools, the explosion and the lying-flat pose. At 3× the character is 60×78 px at 1080p, with a 1 px dark outline.
    • Why not bigger: the walkable floor between the doors is about 1,440 px. 40 characters at 60 px need 2,400 px, so a full crowd overlaps about 1.7 deep but stays readable. At 24×28 (72 px wide) that becomes 2,880 px, about 2 deep, and the heads merge.
    • Why not smaller: 16×24 loses the big-head look and the facial expressions.
    • Recommendation: at 40 characters, show nameplates only for characters who are new, speaking or acting.
  2. Character: a chunky big-head base.
    • The jumpsuit is the platform color (#E5322D/#9C1F1B, #9146FF/#5E2BB0, #53FC18/#2E9A0C), with a yellow #F2C230 collar and cuffs and a dark belt with a buckle.
    • There is no vertical chest stripe and no number.
    • Heads vary across 5 hair colors, a miner helmet with a lamp, and 2 beanies. There are 3 skin tones.
  3. States: 12 states, 4 frames each, following your reference sheet: idle, walk, entrance (materialize), exit (wave, then dematerialize), turn, jump, dig (pickaxe), build (plank and hammer), wave, fall (startled, stumble, tumble, lying flat), explosion and respawn. Walk plays at 7.5 fps and the rest at 5 to 7.5 fps.
  4. Terrain rule: the floor is a row of 16×16 tiles at 3×.
    • Girder tiles have a hazard-stripe top. They are protected and cannot be broken: the entrance, the exit, the respawn area and three bridge sections are girders.
    • Dirt tiles have a dry-grass top and are breakable. Each dig cycle moves a tile one stage, from intact to cracked 1 to cracked 2 to a hole, and rubble chunks fall out. Repair runs the same stages in reverse.
    • Digging on a girder shows the "unavailable" feedback: a clang spark, a red × and a "clang! too solid" bubble.
  5. Doors: a round riveted arch set into a block facade, with pipes and a warm interior lamp. The JOIN door has a green indicator light and the EXIT door an amber one, and the lights blink while in use. The emblem is on the facade and on the flag. The blank signboards on legs carry the JOIN and EXIT text.
  6. Respawn pad: when idle, it is a base with a soft green glow and no ring. When active, a green ring rises with particles and the character materializes. Both states are in the tiles panel.
  7. Props: used sparingly: a flag with the emblem, an antenna, a barrel with a generic radiation mark, a crate, a warning sign, a dead shrub, rocks, a grass tuft, a railing, and a hanging lamp under the catwalk.
  8. Nameplate: a dark rounded tag (#1D1B18 at 90%) with a small pointer, cream Oswald 500 text at 18 px and a 16 px platform badge.
  9. Speech bubble: cream #F6ECD2 with a 3 px #1D1B18 outline and a tail, and dark Oswald 500 text at 22 px, up to 300 px wide. Per the issue, each bubble lasts 4 s and at most 4 show at once. The demo now matches both rules.
  10. Boom: a startled "!!" pose, then an orange cartoon starburst. The character's boots and helmet fly off as props, followed by smoke and orange sparks, with no body parts and no gore. The character respawns on the pad about 1.3 s later.
  11. Crowd cap: the demo shows 10 characters. Production follows the issue: at most 40 visible (30 ranked and 10 rotating), with the rest queued behind the JOIN door. The sizing in item 1 is checked against this cap.
  12. Terrain lifetime: the demo starts repairing holes after about 10 s and crumbles stairs after about 9 s so you can see it happen. Production uses the issue's proposed 30 s.
  13. Optional, out of scope for now: HUD extras such as a viewer or on-screen counter, a command legend bar, and "X joined" / "Y left" toasts. Should any of these be in a later issue?
